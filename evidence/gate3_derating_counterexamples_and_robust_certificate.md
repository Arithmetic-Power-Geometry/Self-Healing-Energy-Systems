Proof sketch within scalar assumptions: g(L,alpha)=alpha*min(pref,imax*(1-r*L))-L decreases with L and increases with alpha for L in [p0,p0+clp] and alpha in [.8,1]. Hence the extremal disturbances occur at (Lmax,alpha_min) and (Lmin,alpha_max). Let U be the larger absolute endpoint disturbance. For m>0,d>0, m f'=g-df gives |f(t)| <= max(|f(0)|,U/d) for all t>=0. This is a conditional sufficient frequency envelope, not a guarantee under unknown inverter derating or physical network behavior.

# Five-gate adversarial research protocol — 2026-10-22

Candidate (NOT established novelty): decision-sufficient observability for safe restoration of uncertain distribution networks.

## Collision warning
Topology reconstruction plus risk-aware reconfiguration under incomplete observability already exists (Sun et al., 2025). Partial-observability graph reinforcement learning already exists (Liu et al., 2025). Incomplete-fault-information restoration already exists (Wang et al., 2025). A generic combination is not novel.

## Gates
1. Landscape: systematic 2020–2026 prior-art search and DOI-verified bibliography; challenge the proposed topic from electrical, information-theoretic, formal, optimization and industrial perspectives.
2. Mathematical separation: precise state/observation/action/safety models, definitions, trivial reductions, counterexamples, theorem proof attempts.
3. Reproducible evidence: obtain licensed IEEE feeder models, baseline implementations, pre-registered experiments, exact seeds, scripts and raw results; distinguish simulation from field claims.
4. Adversarial replication: matched baselines, realistic contingencies, AC power flow and protection limitations, sensitivity/ablation, negative-result audit.
5. Final novelty and Q1 targeting: metadata verification, comprehensive competitor matrix, journal scopes and current quartiles, ethics and limitations; only after explicit approval unlock paper/.

Every prompt ends with commit SHA, exact changed paths, reference count, and pass/fail decision. Never invent experiments or guarantee Q1 acceptance.

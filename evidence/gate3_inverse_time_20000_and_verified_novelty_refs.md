# Inverse-time relay coordination experiment and bibliography update

Executed locally 2026-10-22: 20,000 synthetic primary/backup relay cases, deterministic seed 20261022. Formula t=TDS*0.14/((I/Ipickup)^0.02-1) for I>Ipickup; otherwise infinite trip time. Nominal and 30% derated currents evaluated with an additional 40 ms delay, clearing deadlines and primary/backup coordination margins. Observed: nominal approvals 10695, nominal approvals failing under derating 3208, coordination-margin failures among nominal approvals 402, derating-aware approvals 7656, derating-aware failures 0. Zero robust failures is by construction for the exact assumed derating and delay, not independent hardware validation. Some infinite backup trip times are treated as passing coordination; this must be corrected if backup-trip availability is required.

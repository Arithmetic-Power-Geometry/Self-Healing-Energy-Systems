\subsection{Joint frequency and relay screening with a fail-closed physical gate}
\label{sec:p10-joint}
We evaluated six feeder-derived terminal-current proxy cases (buses 60 and 83, three fault resistances), 80 seeded hypothetical settings per case, and three reset semantics, yielding 1,440 model-relative configurations. The joint screen requires both the sign-aware scalar frequency envelope
\[
\max\!\left\{|f_0|,\left|qf_0+(1-q)u_-/D\right|,
\left|qf_0+(1-q)u_+/D\right|\right\}\le f_{\max},
\quad q=e^{-DT/M},
\]
and finite-time primary and sustained-fault counterfactual backup coordination under independent current derating and breaker-delay corners. The stipulated relay trace has two above-pickup pulses separated by a below-pickup interval, with full reset, hold or linear reset semantics. Frequency inertia, damping, net-power intervals, inverter behavior and relay settings are hypothetical, not calibrated to the feeder or equipment.

The hypothetical frequency screen passes 1,097 of 1,440 configurations, while only 18 pass the joint model. Nominal and common-derating screens respectively generate 233 and ten false approvals relative to the stipulated joint corner oracle. Independent-corner agreement is by construction, not a field reliability estimate. The independent JSON validator checks all nine contingency matrices and the explicit physical-evidence gate.
The feeder artifact does not establish phase-continuous energized paths, switching feasibility, current orientation at installed protective CTs, relay inventory and settings, calibrated inverter current limits, or breaker delays. The physical gate therefore issues \emph{zero} authorizations. The results establish a conditional model-relative certificate and counterexamples, not a physically validated restoration action.

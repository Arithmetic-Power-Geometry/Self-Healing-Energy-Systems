\subsection{Feeder-derived protection proxy audit}\label{sec:p9-proxy}
We evaluated 1,200 seeded hypothetical relay configurations, constructed from six IEEE 123-bus fault-current cases at buses 60 and 83 and 200 relay-setting draws per case. The input magnitudes are conductor-1 terminal-1 current proxies at candidate line boundaries, not verified CT or relay inputs.
\begin{table}[H]\centering\caption{Constant-current corner-oracle confusion matrix (1,200 configurations).}\label{tab:p9-corners}
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}\toprule
Screen & TP & FP & FN & TN\\\midrule
Nominal & 24 & 189 & 0 & 987\\
Common derating & 24 & 7 & 0 & 1169\\
Independent box & 24 & 0 & 0 & 1176\\
Full-reset trace & 16 & 25 & 8 & 1151\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}\end{table}
\begin{table}[H]\centering\caption{Full-reset trace-oracle confusion matrix (1,200 configurations).}\label{tab:p9-traces}
\begin{tabular}{lrrrr}\toprule
Screen & TP & FP & FN & TN\\\midrule
Nominal & 41 & 172 & 0 & 987\\
Common derating & 16 & 15 & 25 & 1144\\
Independent box & 16 & 8 & 25 & 1151\\
Full-reset trace & 41 & 0 & 0 & 1159\\\bottomrule
\end{tabular}\end{table}
The independent-box screen matches the constant-current independent-corner oracle by monotonicity and construction, but eight approved configurations fail the distinct full-reset trace model. The latter incorporates a below-pickup interval that resets inverse-time timer progress. These are model-relative counterexamples, not empirical device failure rates.

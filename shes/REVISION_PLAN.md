# SHES — twelve-stage research revision plan

1. Archive baseline and audit repository/workflows.
2. Audit model assumptions, units and frequency theorem; finite-horizon bound.
3. Formalize joint certificate and separate inverse-time versus time-varying pickup semantics.
4. Implement/test definite-time, inverse-time integrator and partial-reset relay models.
5. Reproduce synthetic tests and reconcile all contingency tables.
6. Repair OpenDSS CI and verify generated JSON artifacts.
7. Generate feeder-derived fault currents for switching actions.
8. Calibrate uncertainty intervals and fault-location relay assignments.
9. Test dynamic frequency and cold-load pickup in independently checked models.
10. Benchmark competing screening methods, sensitivities and runtime.
11. Verify all bibliographic metadata and precise novelty statements.
12. Assemble final LaTeX/PDF, reproducibility archive and independent critical audit.

Evidence rules: keep synthetic, static feeder, dynamic and real hardware claims distinct; never invent new measured results. The 100/100 aspiration is a target, not a guarantee.

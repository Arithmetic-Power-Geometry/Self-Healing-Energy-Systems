\subsection{Final scope of the finite-corner evidence}
\label{sec:p12-final}
For an action \(a\) and uncertainty set \(\Omega\), the proposed conditional authorization requires that frequency, protection, and switching predicates all hold for \emph{every} \(\omega\in\Omega\). Such a conjunction is sufficient for the stipulated model requirements only if each component has actually been verified over its quantified domain. The sign-aware first-order frequency envelope and monotone constant-current inverse-time screen admit restricted analytical certificates. The interrupted-current, reset-dependent feeder-proxy experiment instead checks a finite set of uncertainty corners; its 18 model approvals do not prove safety throughout a continuous uncertainty box. An additional 1,458 interior samples without observed violations constitute falsification testing, not a continuum-wide theorem. No physical authorization follows because phase-specific energized connectivity, switching feasibility, CT orientation, installed relay settings, inverter current-limit dynamics, and calibrated breaker delays remain unverified. We make no field-failure-probability or universal novelty claim.
